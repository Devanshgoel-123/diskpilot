DiskPilot 0.6.5 opens faster and feels quicker on every page, and asks for your email once so you can hear about updates.

## Download

**[DiskPilot.dmg](https://github.com/Devanshgoel-123/diskpilot/releases/download/v0.6.5/DiskPilot.dmg)**. One universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or later. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Coming from 0.6.0 or later: DiskPilot shows a banner when the update is ready; install it with one click.

## What's changed

### Faster

- **Opening the app:** the previous scan now loads in the background, so the window is ready straight away instead of waiting for it.
- **Storage:** the folder table draws only the rows on screen, so switching to it with a thousand folders takes about a third of the time it did.
- **Previews:** images open about twice as fast and are a seventh of the size. The small thumbnail shows at once while the full one loads, and thumbnails are kept in `~/Library/Caches/dev.diskpilot.app/thumbnails` (at most 48 MB) so they come back instantly.
- **Less work at startup:** the Docker, Developer Tools, Settings and Assistant pages load the first time you open them, and Files by type fetches each type's files only when it is shown. The app starts with 12% less JavaScript and receives less than half as much data after a scan.

### Email signup

The first time 0.6.5 opens, it asks for your email address. Many people are still on old versions of DiskPilot, and until now there was no way to tell them a fix was out or to ask what they would like changed.

- What is sent, once: your email, the DiskPilot version, the macOS version, and whether the Mac has Apple silicon or Intel. Nothing about your files, folders or scans.
- If the Mac is offline, DiskPilot opens anyway and sends it the next time it starts.
- The email is not sold, shared or used for ads. To change or delete it, write to devanshgoel.dev@gmail.com.
- Settings → Privacy shows the address you signed up with. Full details are on [diskpilot.live/privacy](https://diskpilot.live/privacy).

### Smaller changes

- **Empty Trash:** while DiskPilot measures what is in the Trash, the size and number of files count up as each item is measured, so a big Trash no longer looks stuck on a spinner.
- **Largest items:** double-click a folder, or press Return, to open it in Storage. For a file, it opens the folder that holds it.
