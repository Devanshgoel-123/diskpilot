DiskPilot 0.4.2 gives the menu bar item a new icon that shows how full your disk is, and makes the optional assistant easier to find.

## Download

**[DiskPilot.dmg](https://github.com/Devanshgoel-123/diskpilot/releases/download/v0.4.2/DiskPilot.dmg)**. One universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or later.

To update, quit DiskPilot, then drag the new version into Applications and replace the old one. Your history, settings and saved results are kept. If macOS says it cannot verify the developer, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and select **Open Anyway** once.

## What's changed

### Menu bar

- The menu bar icon is now a ring around DiskPilot's goggles that fills as your startup disk fills, so you can see how full the disk is without opening anything. It updates in the background and follows the light or dark menu bar.

### Assistant

- After a scan, Overview mentions the optional assistant and links straight to its settings. It answers questions such as "What can I safely clean up?" using your own Anthropic API key, kept in the macOS Keychain. It can only read DiskPilot's results, and anything it suggests opens in Cleanup for you to review. Choose **Not now** to hide the note for good.
- The assistant stays off until you turn it on, and nothing changes for people who don't use it.
