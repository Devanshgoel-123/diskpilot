DiskPilot 0.6.2 lists much smaller files in Files by type, so thousands of photos and screenshots that add up can be reviewed and cleared.

## Download

**[DiskPilot.dmg](https://github.com/Devanshgoel-123/diskpilot/releases/download/v0.6.2/DiskPilot.dmg)**. One universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or later. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Coming from 0.6.0 or 0.6.1: DiskPilot shows a banner when the update is ready; install it with one click.

## What's changed

### Files by type

- Files of 25 KB or more are now listed, up to 2,000 per type. Until now only files of 1 MB or more were, so a folder of 2,000 photos could show just a few dozen of them.
- A note above the list says which files are shown and why the rest are not: smaller than 25 KB, or beyond the 2,000 largest. Every file still counts in the type's total.
- Rescan once after updating to see the smaller files. Results saved by earlier versions list only files of 1 MB or more.
