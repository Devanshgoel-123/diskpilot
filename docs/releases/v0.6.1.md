DiskPilot 0.6.1 lets you act on a file straight from its preview: select it for the Trash, or open it in its own app.

## Download

**[DiskPilot.dmg](https://github.com/Devanshgoel-123/diskpilot/releases/download/v0.6.1/DiskPilot.dmg)**. One universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 11 or later. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

On 0.6.0, DiskPilot offers this update itself: click Update in the banner, or check now in Settings > Updates. Coming from 0.5.1 or earlier: quit DiskPilot, then drag the new version into Applications and replace the old one. Your history, settings and saved results are kept.

## What's changed

### See before you remove

- In the preview, in Files by type or Duplicates, Select for Trash adds the file to your selection, and pressing it again takes it out. Command-Delete does the same. Nothing moves to the Trash until you review your selection, as before. The copy you are keeping in a duplicate group cannot be selected.
- Click the large image, or press Command-O, to open the file in its own app: Preview for photos, QuickTime for videos. DiskPilot opens only photos, videos, audio and documents it listed, never apps, scripts, installers, disk images or archives. For those, use Show in Finder.
