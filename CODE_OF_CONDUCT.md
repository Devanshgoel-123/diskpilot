# Code of Conduct

DiskPilot adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the people who use DiskPilot. Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel the project uses).

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile, or open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/Devanshgoel-123/diskpilot/security/advisories/new) if no other channel is suitable. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem harmful, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
