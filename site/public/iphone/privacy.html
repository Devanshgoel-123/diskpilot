Privacy Policy for DiskPilot for iPhone

Last updated 20 November 2026

DiskPilot for iPhone helps you see what fills your iPhone's photo library and free up space. Everything happens on your iPhone. DiskPilot collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere.

What the app reads

  • Your photos and videos, only after you allow access. DiskPilot reads each item's size, dimensions, date, and whether it is a screenshot, a burst photo or a favourite. It looks at small previews to find similar shots. All of this happens on your iPhone.
  • Files in a folder you pick in the Files app, only when you choose one. DiskPilot reads their names, sizes and dates.
  • Your iPhone's free and total storage, to show how full it is.

What the app does not do

  • It does not upload your photos, videos, files or any information about them. The app makes no network connections.
  • It has no account, no sign-in and no analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • It does not share or sell any data, because it has none to share.

What the app stores

DiskPilot keeps one setting on your iPhone: how many items you have deleted with it. The bin of items you swiped away lives only while the app is open. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Deleting photos and files

DiskPilot never deletes anything without asking. You confirm in the app, then iOS asks again. Deleted photos and videos go to Recently Deleted in Photos, and deleted files go to Recently Deleted in Files, where they can be recovered for 30 days. If you use iCloud Photos, deleting a photo also removes it from iCloud and your other devices.

Purchases

The current version is free and offers no purchases. If a paid upgrade is added later, payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. DiskPilot never sees your payment details.

Children

DiskPilot collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
