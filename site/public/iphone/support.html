DiskPilot for iPhone: Support

DiskPilot finds what fills your photo library: large videos, duplicates, similar shots, screenshots and burst photos. You swipe left to put something in the bin and right to keep it. Nothing is deleted until you empty the bin.

Common questions

DiskPilot shows nothing. It needs access to your photos. Open Settings, then DiskPilot, then Photos, and choose Full Access. With Limited Access, DiskPilot only sees the photos you selected.

I deleted something by mistake. Open Photos, then Albums, then Recently Deleted, and tap Recover. Files are in the Files app under Recently Deleted. Both keep items for 30 days.

I deleted photos, but my storage did not go down. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Empty Recently Deleted to free the space now.

I use iCloud Photos. What happens when I delete? iCloud Photos keeps your devices in sync, so a photo deleted on your iPhone is also removed from iCloud and your other devices.

Why do sizes look larger than the space used? With Optimize iPhone Storage on, DiskPilot shows the size of the full originals, some of which are kept in iCloud rather than on the phone.

Can DiskPilot clean WhatsApp, Instagram or other apps? No. iOS keeps each app's data private to that app. The in-app guide "Free up more space" shows where to do it yourself.
